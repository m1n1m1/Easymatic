Easymatic is a free automation app for Android with AI built in. You tell it what your phone should do and when, and it runs in the background, even with the app closed. No account, no ads, no subscription.

What's in it

Over 180 blocks for location, schedules, NFC tags, sensors, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, battery, calls, SMS, notifications, calendar, email, photos, files, media, volume, flashlight, screenshots, text to speech, HTTP and more. You set everything up by dragging blocks onto a canvas and connecting them, so you can see the whole automation at a glance instead of digging through menus.

AI

You can connect Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, OpenRouter or a model you run yourself. Use it to summarise mails, describe photos, transcribe voice messages or write replies. It can also use your own blocks as tools, so it can send messages, set reminders or switch lights for you. You choose what it is allowed to use.

There is also an assistant that builds automations for you. Type what you want and it puts the blocks on the canvas. You can change anything it made afterwards.

Smart home

Home Assistant, MQTT and Philips Hue are supported out of the box. Anything with an HTTP API works too.

Also included

• Widgets and shortcuts to start a macro from the home screen
• A JavaScript block for the special cases
• Import and export of macros
• Plugins, so other apps can add their own blocks
• Other apps can start your macros if you allow it
• Offline translation
• Macros keep working after a reboot

Privacy

There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. Your macros stay on your phone. Data only leaves it when one of your macros sends it somewhere you set up yourself, like your Home Assistant, your mail server or your AI provider.

Easymatic is open source (MIT) and available in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese.

Please note: this is an early alpha. Some things are still rough and some macros may need to be rebuilt in later versions. If something doesn't work or you are missing a block, open an issue on GitHub.
